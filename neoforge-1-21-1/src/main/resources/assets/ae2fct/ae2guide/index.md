# AE2 Fluid Crafting Terminal

AE2FCT adds virtual-fluid crafting support to the existing AE2 terminals. Virtual fluids represent ME fluids in bucket-sized crafting units, so supported bucket recipes can be filled from ME storage instead of keeping buckets in the grid.
