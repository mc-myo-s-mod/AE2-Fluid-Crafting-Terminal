# Terminal Fluid Interact Card
<ItemImage id="ae2fct:terminal_fluid_interact_card" scale={3}/>


The <ItemLink id="ae2fct:terminal_fluid_interact_card" /> enables direct interaction fluids without bucket.  
This means supported bucket recipes can use ME fluids at the <ItemLink id="ae2:crafting_terminal"/> without placing a bucket in the grid.  
Direct filled-bucket ingredients and supported item-tag bucket ingredients can be matched this way. Recipes listed in the [configuration blacklist](configuration.md) still require the original real filled bucket.
